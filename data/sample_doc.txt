IBM Technical Consultant - AI Integration Role Overview

As a Technical Consultant specializing in AI Integration, professionals implement,
configure, and customize AI-driven solutions that integrate seamlessly into
enterprise applications and workflows. This role requires translating solution
designs and architectural blueprints into working systems, ensuring secure,
efficient, and compliant deployment of AI services, APIs, and orchestration layers.

Key Responsibilities:
1. Implement AI Solutions: Develop connectors, build Retrieval-Augmented Generation
   (RAG) pipelines, and configure prompts and guardrails to integrate AI services
   into enterprise applications and workflows.
2. Collaborate with Stakeholders: Work closely with architects, data engineers,
   and business stakeholders to validate requirements, deliver proofs of concept,
   and harden solutions for production.
3. Optimize Performance: Implement observability, performance tuning, and cost
   optimization measures to ensure reliable and scalable AI solutions.
4. Ensure Compliance: Deploy AI services, APIs, and orchestration layers in
   compliance with organizational and regulatory standards.
5. Deliver Solutions: Translate solution designs and architectural blueprints
   into working systems, focusing on reliability, scalability, and responsible
   AI principles throughout the delivery lifecycle.

Required Education: Bachelor's Degree
Preferred Education: Master's Degree

Required Technical Expertise:
- Exposure to API Integration
- Experience with Workflow Automation and event-driven patterns
- LLMOps Practices including observability and performance tuning
- Secure Deployment experience
